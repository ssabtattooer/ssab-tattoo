Artist Profile

SSAB

Seoul-based tattoo artist and owner / lead artist of Mama Ink Studio. With 16 years of tattooing experience, SSAB works primarily in Japanese Irezumi and Black & Grey, from focused individual pieces to full sleeves, backs and other large-scale compositions.

The work is developed around placement, body flow and long-term readability rather than simply reproducing an image on skin.
